Pacing

Steam lets an account leave about 10 comments a day on people who aren't its friends. Go past that and Steam quietly stops accepting comments from that account for around a day. It isn't a ban and nothing is broken, but nothing you post gets through either — and rep4rep will never credit it. nocat.farm keeps every account under the line, spaces the comments out at random, and only posts during the hours you allow.

These live on rep4rep.com, because their API can't do them: removing a Steam profile · editing your custom comment list · task history · buying points · referrals. Open rep4rep.com ↗
